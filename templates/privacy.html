Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Spendly collects when you use the app, how that information is used, and the choices you have. Spendly is a personal expense tracking app, and we aim to collect only what is needed to make it work.

1. Data We Collect

We collect the information you give us directly:

  • Account details — your name, email address, and password when you register.
  • Expense records — the amount, category, date, and description of each expense you add.

We do not ask for your bank account, card numbers, or access to any of your financial accounts. Everything in Spendly is entered by you.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your information to:

  • Create your account and sign you in.
  • Store your expenses and show them back to you.
  • Calculate the totals, category breakdowns, and summaries you see in the app.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your expense data for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your account details and expense records are stored in the Spendly database and kept for as long as your account exists. You can edit or delete individual expenses at any time.

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, but no method of storing data is completely secure. Please choose a strong password and keep it to yourself.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly loads its fonts from Google Fonts. When you open a page, your browser connects to Google's servers to download them, and Google may receive technical information such as your IP address. Your expense data is not sent to Google.

Spendly loads its icons from unpkg, a public file host. Your browser connects to unpkg's servers to download them, and unpkg may receive the same kind of technical information. Your expense data is not sent to unpkg.

Apart from this, we do not share your information with third parties, unless we are required to do so by law.

5. Contact Us

If you have a question about this Privacy Policy or about how your data is handled, write to us at privacy@spendly.example and we will get back to you.
